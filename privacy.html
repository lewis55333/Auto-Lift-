Privacy information

This is a draft privacy notice and must be completed by Auto Lift before the website is published. It is not a substitute for checking your legal obligations.

Information you may provide

The enquiry form is designed to collect your name, telephone number, optional email address, service requested, collection location, destination and details about your vehicle or situation.

Why the information is requested

Use enquiry information only to respond to the request, discuss availability and pricing, and arrange a service if agreed. Do not collect information you do not need.

What must be completed before launch

  • Add the correct legal business/operator name and contact details.
  • Confirm the lawful basis for handling enquiries and how long enquiry records are retained.
  • Explain who can access enquiry data and any service providers used to process it, including website form hosting.
  • Provide a contact route for privacy requests and explain applicable rights.
  • Check cookie, analytics, advertising and data-protection requirements for the final setup.

Do not publish this draft unchanged. Replace it with a complete notice that accurately reflects how Auto Lift handles personal data.
